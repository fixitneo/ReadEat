Readest Personal page sounds

Three prerecorded paper page-turn effects are obtained from Mixkit SFX during the APK build, and bundled ONLY into the final end-user app.

paper: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1104/1104-preview.mp3
book: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1105/1105-preview.mp3
soft: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2380/2380-preview.mp3

License: https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/ (Mixkit Sound Effects Free License).

The license permits use of the audio as a component of a larger end product such as a reader app, including private use. It prohibits standalone resale, distribution as stock, or sharing with tool/template source files. Do not redistribute this repository's build outputs as a stand-alone audio library. Source audio must be fetched afresh during builds. Builds fail if the URLs are no longer usable.
